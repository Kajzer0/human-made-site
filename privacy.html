Privacy Policy

What leaves your browser

To decide whether a video or channel carries a mark, the extension sends the YouTube identifiers it can see on the page you already have open (video ids, channel handles and ids) to a database we operate on Supabase, and receives back a verification status.

Some YouTube surfaces, the sidebar next to a video for one, show no channel link at all, only the channel's display name. A display name is not an identifier, since any channel can take someone else's, so for those cards the extension asks YouTube's own public oEmbed endpoint about that single video, a request from your page to YouTube, which already serves that page, and sends only the handle or channel id it answers with. A card whose channel cannot be confirmed that way is left unmarked rather than guessed from the name.

If you choose to report a video or a channel, the report you write (its kind, reason, the identifiers involved, and any note you type) is stored there so a person can review it. Nothing else is transmitted: we do not record the pages you visit, your clicks, your history, or anything that identifies you.

What we keep

Verification records and submitted reports, stored on Supabase. Reports are insert-only from the extension: it cannot read, change, or delete them. Your settings and cached statuses stay in your own browser storage.

What we never do

We do not sell or share data, we do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not ask for personal information. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
