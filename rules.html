How verification works

What the Human Made mark means and how the content is verified.

What the badge means

A mark says one thing: “This video, or this channel, was made by people”.

It is not a statement about quality, and it is not an endorsement.

This is an allowlist, so an unverified video simply hasn’t been checked yet. (Submit it for review. Pretty please 🙏. The more people contribute, the quicker we can make YT clean of mass produced soulless AI bullshit).

What earns a badge

A general rule for the video to even be considered for verification is that at least ~90% of the entire work put into it has to be done by a real, breathing person.

  • Tools do not disqualify.
    1. Removing a background in Photoshop,
    2. Cleaning recorded audio,
    3. Transcribing your own speech,
    4. Or using some sort of minor feature that happens to be model powered is okay.
    As long as AI is used as a helpful tool and not a replacement for real work, it’s probably fine.
  • Generated work DOES disqualify.
    1. Using TTS that’s supposed to sound like a real human and is used out of laziness and not as a channel gimmick,
    2. AI b-roll footage or images passed off as real,
    3. Generated scripts with only minor adjustments
    4. AI music that is the main point of the video with no added value,
    5. Or some “Hey ChatGPT, make me a Mr Beast thumbnail” type stuff.
    Generally speaking, if you’re replacing the core creative voice, face, editing, or writing with an AI generator just to cut corners, you won’t be verified.
  • A small slip can be ignored. Twenty seconds of generated voice or a few short b-rolls in a thirty-minute, high-quality, high-effort video does not completely cross you out of the list.

Badge can sometimes be wrong (tragic)

Since I don’t have a mind control machine (yet), I cannot guarantee that a channel that was once verified didn’t enter the dark path since that time. If you see a channel with a badge that has become AI garbage, report it for re-check.

Scenario 1:
Channel X has 1000 great and high-quality videos.
Channel X makes 1 AI-made dumpster-level video.
Some ✨wonderful✨ person reports this video.
Channel X gets onto my FBI watchlist and if this happens again, it will be kicked out of the cool kids club.

Scenario 2:
Channel Y has 10 videos of questionable quality.
Someone submits Channel Y for a cool Human Made badge.
Channel Y gets left on seen.
We laugh at channel Y silently (or not so silently) and judge it from afar.

Scenario 3:
Channel Z was a low-quality doo doo before.
Channel Z since then has become an absolute master of their craft.
Channel Z for the last 2 years has been uploading absolute cinema.
We forgive Channel Z for being a doo doo and only care about the latest uploads.
Everyone happy.

How a decision is made

  1. A channel is submitted for review by some kind soul.
  2. A person watches a sample of videos from said channel.
  3. Then one of two things happens:
    1. Channel has soul → Verified
    2. Channel doesn’t have soul → Ignored

How to argue with me

Open the panel on any video page. If the fingerprint is green and you disagree with it, report it and briefly explain what smelled funny in there.

If you know a channel that for 100000% has a human behind it and it doesn’t have a badge yet, suggest it (seriously like… we all need that data bro).

A voting system where the answer is not so clear is planned, but for now I don’t have any users, so like, whatever.
