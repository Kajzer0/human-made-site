A mark for videos made by people.

Human Made · browser extension for YouTube · version 0.2.0

Human Made adds a fingerprint badge to YouTube videos whose creators have been verified for not making low effort AI bullshit.

What the badge means

  • A channel with a badge has been verified to have a real breathing person behind it.
  • A channel with no badge means the channel has either been ignored or not checked yet.

How a video earns the mark

We judge the making, not the topic. A tech channel about AI can get verified, but a channel generated by AI, obviously, cannot. The full rules are on The Rules page.

Privacy

No personal data is being collected (bc what for honestly? Google spyware is enough). No cookies, no blah blah. For now the addon only asks Supabase whether a video you stumble upon is from a verified channel or not. Nothing leaves your browser unless you submit a channel for review. More details in professionally and lawyerly written privacy policy.
