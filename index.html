A mark for videos made by people.

Human Made · browser extension for YouTube · version 0.2.0

Human Made adds a small to YouTube videos whose creators we have verified as human-made, so you can tell at a glance what you are watching.

What the badge means

  • A filled badge means the video or its channel has been checked and confirmed as human-made.
  • A badge from a name match looks the same and rests on less: some YouTube surfaces show only a channel's display name, which anyone can copy. Its description and the panel say so in words, and we never guess when two verified channels share a name.
  • No badge means we have nothing to say about the video.

How a video earns the mark

We judge the making, not the subject. A channel about AI can carry the mark, a channel that generated its video cannot. The full rules, including what is still undecided, are on the verification page.

What it does not do

No account, no login, no cookies, no analytics. The extension asks a database we operate about the identifiers of the video or channel on the page you already have open, and nothing else leaves your browser unless you report something yourself. The details are in the privacy policy.
